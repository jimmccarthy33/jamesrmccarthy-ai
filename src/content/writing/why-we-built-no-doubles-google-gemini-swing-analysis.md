When Bryson DeChambeau and Google announced the Google Gemini Swing Analysis tool, a lot of golfers saw a cool demo.

I saw something different.

I saw confirmation.

Confirmation that the exact technology we have been building — the same underlying pose tracking, the same AI reasoning layer, the same idea of simple upload and clear improvement — is the direction the entire golf world is about to move in.

But the truth is, No Doubles did not start because of Google Gemini.  
It did not start because of Bryson.  
And it did not start as a business idea at all.

It started because I wanted to build something meaningful with my son.

We both love golf.  
We both wanted to get better.  
And unlike the Google Gemini Swing Analysis tool — which arrived with a massive press rollout — our project started simply.

What if an average golfer could upload a swing and get one clear answer: what is wrong and how to fix it?

Not hype.  
Not noise.  
Not swing grades.  
Just clarity.

---

## **Where No Doubles Actually Started**

I dealt with the same frustrations every golfer deals with:

Too many tips  
Contradicting advice  
No practice plan  
Not knowing which flaw matters  
Feeling like you are guessing every time you stand over the ball

Nothing told me:

Here is the one thing costing you strokes, and here is the drill that fixes it.

So we started building it ourselves — not as a startup, but as a project between a father and son.

And that is how No Doubles began.

---

## **How We Accidentally Built Something Big**

The idea was small.  
The build was not.

We drifted into building an entire golf improvement ecosystem:

A swing flaw taxonomy  
A mapped drill library  
A motor learning based curriculum  
A custom video upload pipeline  
Pose extraction and telemetry  
Frame synced overlays  
A multi agent AI analysis system  
A curriculum engine  
Community features  
Coaching pathways

It got big — far bigger than we intended.

At some point we had to stop and ask:

What does the average golfer actually need?

Not complexity.  
Not ten new features.  
Not a giant platform.

Just clear, daily, predictable improvement.

So we focused everything around one mission.

Fix the flaw.  
Give the right drill.  
Build the habit.  
Eliminate doubles.

---

## **And Then Google Gemini Released Their Swing Analysis Tool**

People will Google this, so we should talk about it honestly.

When the Google Gemini Golf Swing Analysis demo dropped with Bryson, my first reaction was simple:

Gut punch.

This is the biggest company in the world, paired with one of golf’s biggest personalities, showing technology that looked a lot like what we were building.

But that feeling lasted about thirty seconds.

The next thought was:

Validation.

Because the architecture they showed —  
video upload,  
pose landmarks,  
AI reasoning,  
corrections,  
explanations —

It is the same system we developed from scratch.

We were not copying them.  
We were parallel.

And here is the difference that matters.

Google and Bryson built a tech demo.  
No Doubles is building a daily improvement system for normal golfers.

We are not trying to be flashy.  
We are trying to be useful.
