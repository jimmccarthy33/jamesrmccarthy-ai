Thank you for being part of our beta and helping us build No Doubles into the most honest, structured, and useful improvement system in golf. Every swing you upload matters. Every bit of feedback helps us make the product smarter and more consistent. You’re directly shaping how this platform evolves.

Before we get into everything that’s new, one important note:

---

## **⟳ A Heads-Up on Swing Score Variance**

Over the next few weeks, you may notice some swings scoring higher or lower than expected.  
That’s intentional.

We’re tuning the analysis engine — balancing flaw detection, adjusting severity models, and improving root-cause logic. As these changes roll out, scores will move around a bit. That’s part of getting to a more accurate, more trustworthy scoring system.

If your score changes during this period, it’s simply the engine recalibrating itself to get closer to the truth.

---

## **✔ Improvements We’ve Made Since Launch**

These updates came directly from your swings and your feedback. Thank you — this is how we improve fast.

---

## **1. Five-Swing Snapshot for Real Patterns**

You can now select any five analyzed swings to get a clearer, more reliable read on your true tendencies.

You’ll see:

- Your dominant flaw
- Repeatable patterns
- A more stable swing quality score
- Better direction for what to work on next

Golfers don’t have 50 problems. They have a couple of patterns that repeat.  
Snapshot now reflects that reality.

---

## **2. More Transparent, More Actionable Scoring**

We refined the scoring engine to focus on clarity and accuracy, not noise:

- Better flaw severity weighting
- More consistent penalties
- Clear explanations of what affects your score
- More meaningful interpretation of flaws that impact ball flight

The goal: fewer surprises, more “this makes sense” moments.

---

## **3. Training Plans: Present, Improving, Still Early**

Training Plans are live — but they’re still in the early, evolving stage.

Here’s what’s improved:

- Daily blocks load reliably
- Drills appear consistently
- Navigation is smoother
- Plans better reflect your Snapshot patterns

**And here’s the honest truth:**  
Training Plans are not the primary focus of the beta yet. They’re functional, but still raw. We’re actively improving the structure, flow, and logic — and your feedback here helps us tighten them.

The main purpose of the beta is crystal clear:

**Get swings. Improve the AI. Build a foundation of accuracy and trust.**

Training Plans will become much stronger as the analysis engine continues to improve.

---

## **4. Faster, More Polished App Experience**

We tightened the UI to make the app feel more stable and predictable:

- Faster loading across key screens
- More reliable Snapshot responsiveness
- Thumbnail stability
- Cleaned-up dialogs and modals
- Consistent branding across pages

Not flashy changes — just the kind that make the product feel more solid and less beta-like.

---

## **5. Signup & Password Reset Fixes**

Account creation and password reset are now fully stable and consistently reliable after resolving backend issues that impacted new users.

---

## **🚧 What We’re Working on Next**

These are the major initiatives in development — and the ones that will shape the next leap forward.

---

## **1. Version 3 of Our Swing Analysis Engine**

V3 is the biggest upgrade we’re building right now.

It moves the system from identifying **what happened** in your swing to explaining **why it happened** — and connecting that cause to the drill that actually fixes the pattern.

This includes:

- More accurate flaw detection
- More consistent scoring
- Early use of your mobility limitations
- Better root-cause mapping
- Stronger coaching logic built into the engine

This is the foundation the entire platform depends on.

---

## **2. Smarter, Better Connected Training Plans**

As the analysis engine improves, Training Plans will evolve from “functional but early” into something more structured and more tailored:

- Tighter integration with your Snapshot
- Better week-to-week progression
- Clearer practice flow
- More No Doubles–specific drills
- Stronger logic behind what you work on and why

We want Training Plans to eventually feel like your personal roadmap.  
Right now, they’re a good start — and improving quickly.

---

## **3. Expanded Drill Library — And We’d Love Your Help**

We’re building more drills rooted in motor learning, pattern correction, and real-world transfer.

If you have:

- A range
- A hitting bay
- A simulator
- Or interest in helping record drills

—we’d love to collaborate. Reach out through support and we’ll connect.

More real drills recorded in real environments will accelerate this entire system.

---

## **Thank You**

Your swings and your feedback are helping us build something golfers have needed for a long time: a clear, honest, structured path to improvement.

We’re grateful you’re here and excited for what’s coming next.
